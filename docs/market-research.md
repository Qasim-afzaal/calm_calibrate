## 2. The Problem (Why People Search & Pay)

### Problem scale

| Problem | Prevalence |
|---------|------------|
| Neck pain among office workers | ~53% report regularly |
| Lower back pain | ~53% |
| Shoulder pain | ~52% |
| Prolonged sitting | ~82% sit most of the workday (often 7–9+ hours) |
| MSK pain in IT/desk jobs | ~66% pooled prevalence (meta-analysis) |

### Jobs-to-be-done (what users actually want)

Users are not buying “stretching.” They are buying:

1. **Pain relief** — “My neck/back hurts after 6 hours at the desk”
2. **Prevention** — “I don’t want chronic issues at 35”
3. **Energy & focus** — “I crash at 3pm”
4. **Habit formation** — “I know I should move but I forget”
5. **Desk-specific routines** — “Gym apps don’t fit my office/chair”

### Employer angle

Musculoskeletal (MSK) issues drive absenteeism, healthcare costs, and lost productivity. Employers increasingly fund wellness tools with clear ROI — a strong B2B path alongside consumer subscriptions.

## 4. Competitive Landscape

### How many apps exist?

| Category | Approx. count | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Posture-related apps | 250+ (Google Play keyword “postures”) | Many low quality |
| Stretching apps (broader) | ~15,000 listed | Crowded — Bend, StretchIt, etc. |
| Desk-specific movement apps | ~15–30 serious players | **Your real competitive set** |

### Direct competitors

| App | Focus | Platform | Pricing | Traction |
|-----|-------|----------|---------|----------|
| **Wakeout** | Fun 30-sec desk movements | iOS, Mac, Chrome ext | ~$48–60/yr | Apple App of the Year, 4.8★, 10M+ sessions |
| **Moova** | Break reminders + guided stretches | iOS, Android | ~$40–55/yr | 4.8★, 1.7K+ ratings |
| **Exercise Snacks** | Science-backed micro-breaks | iOS | ~£50/yr | Newer, strong positioning |
| **Bend** | General stretching (not desk-specific) | iOS, Android | Freemium | 10M+ users |
| **Stretchly** | Desktop break reminders | Win, Mac, Linux | Free (open source) | Timers only — no guided content |
| **WorkRave** | RSI prevention | Win, Linux | Free | Established but dated |
| **Keybreak** | Mac/Win stretch reminders | Desktop | Paid | Background nudges |
| **Neomove** | B2B physio-coach for sedentary work | Mobile + enterprise | €6–7.90/employee/mo | Corporate channel |
| **Ergomate** | Desk ergonomics + habits | Mobile + portal | €3–15/desk/mo | B2B desk management |

### Competitive gaps CalmCalibrate can exploit

1. **Desk-native, pain-first** — Most apps are generic fitness or fun movement; few own “neck/back pain at 4pm”
2. **Cross-platform mobile + desktop** — Wakeout is strong on Apple; Android + desktop companion is underserved
3. **Science-backed micro-doses** — 60–90 second routines with measurable relief (Exercise Snacks proves this works)
4. **B2B MSD prevention** — Employers pay for anonymized team wellness dashboards

## 6. Business Models

### Model 1: B2C subscription (most common)

```
Free:     3–5 routines, basic reminders
Pro:      $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr — full library, personalization, streaks
```

**Benchmark:** Wakeout $48–60/yr · Moova $40–55/yr · Exercise Snacks ~£50/yr  
**Target:** 10,000 paying users × $50/yr = **$500K ARR**

### Model 2: B2B / corporate wellness (higher LTV)

```
PEPM:     $3–8 per employee per month
Includes: Anonymized pain/engagement dashboard for HR
```

**Examples:** Neomove €6/employee/mo · Ergomate €3–15/desk/mo  
**Target:** 50 companies × 200 employees × $5/mo = **$600K ARR**

### Model 3: Freemium + premium programs

- Free: reminders + limited exercises
- Paid: pain-specific programs (neck, back, hips), progress analytics

### Recommended: Hybrid model

| Channel | Offer |
|---------|-------|
| B2C | Individual subscribers at $40–60/yr |
| B2B | Team licenses for remote-first companies at $4–6/employee/mo |
| Desktop | Mac/Windows companion for break reminders (differentiation) |

## 7. Differentiation Strategy for CalmCalibrate

Don’t be “Bend for desk workers.” Be the **desk worker recovery system**:

| Pillar | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pain-first** | “Neck pain after 4pm?” — not “do a stretch” |
| **Desk-native** | Chair, small space, work clothes — no gym required |
| **Micro-doses** | 60–90 seconds, not 15-minute yoga |
| **Smart timing** | Work hours only; optional calendar / idle detection |
| **Progress** | “Neck mobility improved this week” — tangible feedback |
| **Evidence** | Cite research behind micro-break benefits |

### MVP feature priority

1. Pain selector — neck / back / hips / eyes / general stiffness
2. 60–90 second guided routines (video or animation, no equipment)
3. Smart reminders — respect work hours; no weekend nagging
4. Streak + relief tracking — “How do you feel?” after each break
5. Desktop companion (Phase 2) — Mac/Windows menu bar reminders
