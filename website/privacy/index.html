Summary: CalmCalibrate is built local-first. Your wellness data is stored on your device. We do not require an account, we do not sell personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics in the current version.

1. Overview

CalmCalibrate (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides desk mobility sessions, break reminders, and progress tracking for people who sit during work. This Privacy Policy describes what information the mobile app processes, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to the CalmCalibrate app on iOS and Android and to this website (calm-calibrate.vercel.app), which provides marketing and support information only.

Data controller: CalmCalibrate · support@calcalibrate.com

2. Information we collect

Information is collected through your use of the app and stored locally on your device unless noted below.

2.1 Profile & preferences

  • Display name (optional)
  • Pain areas selected during onboarding (neck, shoulders, upper/lower back, hips)
  • Sitting hours and preferred break times
  • Wellness goals and reminder frequency
  • Theme (light/dark) and language preference
  • Notification and smart-reminder settings

2.2 Activity & wellness data

  • Mobility assessment answers and mobility score
  • Completed session logs, duration, and exercise history
  • Streaks, achievements, and 30-day journey progress
  • Daily check-ins and pre-workout pain ratings
  • Workout mood selections and sound preferences
  • Posture analysis results (when enabled)
  • AI-generated daily plans and weekly insights (when enabled)
  • CalmCalibrate Pro subscription status (stored locally)

2.3 Website

This website is static. We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels on calm-calibrate.vercel.app. Vercel (our hosting provider) may process standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) as described in Vercel's privacy policy.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable under GDPR and similar laws, we rely on:

  • Contract / service delivery: to provide app features you request (sessions, reminders, progress)
  • Consent: for optional permissions (notifications, camera when enabled)
  • Legitimate interests: to improve app stability and respond to support requests

You may withdraw consent for optional features in your device or app settings at any time.

4. How we use information

  • Personalize desk mobility sessions to your pain areas and goals
  • Calculate and display mobility scores and progress charts
  • Schedule local break reminders during your chosen work hours
  • Track streaks, achievements, journey milestones, and weekly recaps
  • Unlock CalmCalibrate Pro features when you subscribe
  • Apply theme, language, and audio preferences

CalmCalibrate is a general wellness app. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Consult a qualified health professional for medical advice.

5. Storage & security

5.1 On-device storage

App data is stored using SQLite and on-device preferences. The app is designed to work offline without syncing wellness data to our servers.

5.2 Security measures

  • Data remains in your app's protected sandbox on iOS and Android
  • We do not operate a central database of user wellness profiles in the current version
  • We recommend enabling your device passcode or biometric lock

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data on your device.

6. Device permissions

Notifications

Optional. Used to deliver local break reminders. Content is generated on-device. Disable anytime in app or system settings.

Exact alarms (Android)

Optional. Allows reminders at precise scheduled times when the app is in the background.

Camera

Optional, when posture scanning is enabled. Video is processed on-device and not uploaded in the current version.

7. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Limited sharing may occur with:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — subscription billing per their policies
  • Hosting (Vercel) — for this static website only
  • Legal obligations — when required by valid legal process
  • Future AI providers — only if you opt in; we will update this policy first

8. Subscriptions & payments

CalmCalibrate Pro is purchased through Apple or Google. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers. Subscription status may be cached locally to unlock Pro features.

Manage or cancel via your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

9. Retention & deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it by:

  • Uninstalling CalmCalibrate
  • Clearing app storage in iOS or Android settings

Because we do not hold server copies of wellness data, we cannot delete it remotely on your behalf in the current version.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data.

  • Access / export: view data in the app's Profile and Progress screens
  • Deletion: uninstall the app or clear storage
  • Object / restrict: disable optional permissions or Pro features
  • Complaint: contact your local data protection authority

For privacy requests: support@calcalibrate.com

11. International users

CalmCalibrate is available globally. Because data stays on your device, cross-border transfers of wellness data to our servers do not occur in the current version. If we add cloud features, we will describe transfer mechanisms and safeguards in an updated policy.

12. Children

CalmCalibrate is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. Material changes may also be noted in the app when applicable.
